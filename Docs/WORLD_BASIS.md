# Booter & BigARM World Basis

This document is the canonical baseline for the game's setting, tone, and core fantasy.

## High Concept

Booter & BigARM is an elevated top-down, fully 3D survival crafting game set on a dead world called the Broken World.

The game is about endurance, deliberate movement, and living inside a hostile system rather than conquering it.

## Core Fantasy

- The player is a vulnerable survivor operating inside a vast, dangerous system.
- Movement, traversal, combat, and interaction are intentional and constrained.
- The experience should feel weighty and grounded, even from a top-down perspective.
- Preparation, attrition, and decision-making matter more than speed or power.

## The Broken World

- The world is a planet-wide canyon system carved into deep chasms.
- There are no oceans, rivers, rain, or plant life.
- Natural liquid water has not existed on the planet for roughly ten thousand years. Dry channels, canyons and sedimentary forms may preserve much older hydrological history, but there is no active natural water cycle or current fluvial landscape process.
- The sky is permanently orange and the world reads in reds, rusts, and iron hues.
- The setting is procedurally generated and effectively infinite.
- Ancient ruins, broken war machines, and buried megastructures appear throughout the terrain.

## Perpetual Twilight

- One planetary pole points toward the sun while the opposite pole points away from it.
- People live around the equatorial boundary between those extremes, where the world remains in perpetual twilight.
- The sun's apparent daily activity has a figure-eight relationship in the world's lore. The game does not need to reproduce that astronomical path literally.
- The playable day/night cycle moves between brighter sunset and deeper twilight. It never becomes conventional noon or full night.
- The low sun angle makes long shadows a stable part of navigation, concealment, danger, and environmental readability rather than a purely visual effect.

## Atmospheric and Planetary Hazards

- The world has windstorms and dense dust storms.
- Electrical storms are violent and dangerous.
- The setting includes a mild, long-duration planetary resonance or shifting-world idea rather than ordinary Earth-style tectonic earthquakes. This is lore context and does not require the world to vibrate during gameplay.
- If an earthquake appears in the game, it will be an explicitly scripted story moment rather than an ambient or recurring simulation.
- Exact storm behavior, the physical cause of the resonance and its narrative meaning remain open for creative development.

## Survival Rules

- There is no water in the world.
- Liquids occur inside living things; extracted faunal chemicals and liquids can be used as materials. This does not establish natural free water or make those liquids drinkable.
- Human survival depends on algae.
- Settlements maintain algae vats.
- People carry personal algae reserves as the backbone of survival.
- The Phagia used for eating and drinking can be processed into higher-performing consumables, including prepared drinks, algae bars, dried bars and prepared meals. Specific recipes, portion sizes, nutritional/hydration benefits, preservation and other game effects remain undefined.
- Selected edible fauna resources can be combined with Phagia to produce high-quality delicacy meals. Phagia remains the nutritional foundation; this culinary use does not make fauna generally nutritious or its industrial chemicals edible.
- Failure should come from accumulation: resource depletion, exposure, distance, and poor planning.

## Faunal Biology And Material Use

Author direction recorded October 14, 2026:

- Some fauna can be eaten, but it is not nutritious. This does not replace algae as the basis of human survival.
- Most fauna does not have flesh in the traditional sense. Do not assume conventional meat, muscle or fat anatomy when designing harvestable resources.
- The internal chemicals of most fauna are toxic to humans. Those chemicals and liquids can nevertheless be used as materials; toxicity and material usefulness are separate properties.
- Species-specific edible fractions, organs, chemical identities, processing uses and extraction/containment rules remain undefined here. Edibility does not imply that other parts of the same creature are safe.
- The author identifies **Burden Beasts** anatomy in [Arc-Dust](https://github.com/sleepyseamonster/Arc-Dust) as a reference. Read-only access was restored October 14: related Native Fauna Biology and Domestic Wasteland Beetle records are source proposals at revision `f6f98515c005dc3c73cccd9cbc031691745740c6`. The exact Burden Beasts owning record is not yet identified. These game rules retain direct author authority; differing source anatomy/textile proposals are not automatically adopted.

## Material Culture

Author direction recorded October 14, 2026:

- Oxygen-releasing stone, glowing stone and flint are recognized resource families. Specific mineral names, activation, output, duration, depletion and behavior after extraction remain undefined. Glowing stone does not automatically imply combustion or a heat source.
- Fire behaves differently in this world. Cooking and fire-related equipment must account for the setting's atmospheric rules. Detailed oxygen, fuel, airflow and cooking behavior remain design work; proposal-stage source mechanisms do not establish implemented game systems.
- Shell husks supply abundant chitin and icore fibers that can be scraped from them. The term `icore` retains the author's spelling; its composition is not yet defined.
- Scraped husk fibers are the main source of paper.
- Ordinary megafauna shells are cheap bulk material. This does not make processed fibers, precision components or labor-intensive finished goods cheap.
- Delicate inner wings from insectoid megafauna can be gathered as resources and used as material for vertical sun shades. Preparation, joining, durability and the actual shade properties remain open.
- Delicate insectoid wings also supply sail material. Wind turbines can be built from fallen/salvaged parts; rotor design, installation and useful mechanical or electrical output remain undefined.
- These fibers can also be processed and pressed into textile materials, but this is very difficult. Abundant raw fibers do not imply easy finished-material production.
- Hides and furs can be processed into textile and other materials, but the usable yield is extremely low. No numeric yield or processing recipe is established.
- There is no cloth of any kind, but clothing can be made from husk fibers, animal fur, hides and related processed materials. The no-cloth rule does not prohibit garments from these sources.
- There is no wood. The author reaffirmed this material restriction on October 15, 2026; timber, lumber and wooden components must not be introduced as ordinary or salvaged game materials.
- Everyday clothing is simple: wraps, metal chainmail and other practical wear that can be made with metal and minimal fur. Minimal fur use does not create an abundant fur-processing supply; wrap construction and fastening details remain open under the no-cloth rule.
- Elaborate fashion clothing takes very large quantities of material and very large amounts of processing and crafting time. Fashion items are consequently very rare and expensive. This fashion scarcity does not make ordinary simple wraps or chainmail inherently luxury items.
- Specialized spectacles, eyeglasses and hats are rare. Their exact optical functions, construction and game effects remain open.
- Inputs used to craft expensive fashion items are intended to command high sale value, giving resource gathering a valuable role in the economy. Exact recipes, input quantities, prices and crafting times remain undefined.
- Seals and sealants are recognized canister-related item/material families. Specific formulations, part compatibility, service procedures and game effects remain undefined; source-lore canister systems are references rather than installed gameplay.
- Gemstones exist on the Broken World. Particular gemstone types, deposits, grades, rarity, cutting practices and uses remain open for brainstorming; gemstone existence does not approve a specific terrestrial mineral roster or special powers.
- Ancient technology uses materials comparable to carbon-fiber composites, nanocarbon materials, plasteels and related futuristic manufactured substances. These materials are **extremely rare**.
- Their exact compositions, properties, recovery forms and present-day manufacturing possibilities remain undefined. Finding an ancient material does not establish the ability to reproduce it.

### Material Refinement Tiers

Author direction clarified October 15, 2026, superseding the earlier material-only classification:

- Tier 0 covers items as found, gathered, harvested or recovered from the world. A complex recovered mechanism can be T0 while its condition is unknown; finding it does not make it ready to use in crafting.
- Tier 1 covers the first craft-ready form after the necessary selection, processing, refining, inspection or repair. An ancient part can reach this readiness stage only if a compatible original survives and can be qualified; this does not establish the ability to reproduce or repair its internal technology.
- Tier 2 products use T1 inputs and can also use T0 inputs. Tier 3 products use T1/T2 inputs; Tier 4 products use T2/T3 inputs. Lower-tier supporting materials can accompany the required stage. Finished gear, materials and components all receive numeric planning tiers; rarity, original technological complexity, quality and item type remain separate classifications.
- Products can finish at an earlier tier without a forced four-tier chain. As-found and qualified forms need distinct condition records when their crafting eligibility differs. Refilling, charging or packing existing items must preserve their physical identity and cannot create extra holders, contents or energy.
- The working [master item database in Google Sheets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/18Fw8qhdgbb-w_Y4L3iPn2NW7ag6nKWyE-AVYZ45ck5o/edit) records current tier assignments, source conditions and candidate routes. The [local workbook](./Design/Items/MASTER_ITEM_DATABASE.xlsx) is a separate retained artifact; synchronization must be deliberate. Grouping several preparation operations within a readiness stage, particular assignments, recipes and regional demand/fashion ideas remain design proposals. A numeric planning tier does not resolve a withheld biological premise, source uncertainty, operating performance or present-day ancient-material manufacture.

### Material Mark Currencies

Author direction recorded October 15, 2026:

- Currency includes iron marks, steel marks, ceramic marks and glass marks. Material and craftsmanship distinguish the currency items; this does not assign them a material-refinement tier.
- Exchange ratios, denomination ranking, craftsmanship grades, issuing authority and the exact relationship to the source proposal's Imperial Commons remain undefined. The database records the four material marks separately without inventing those details.

### Machine-Derived Communications

Author direction recorded October 14, 2026:

- Crude two-way radios exist and were reverse engineered from machine technology.
- Their construction, power supply, transmission range, availability and behavior in the world's charged atmosphere remain undefined. This establishes a lore item family, not an implemented radio system or a particular crafting tier.

### Ancient Weapons And Reverse-Engineered Machinery

Author direction recorded October 14, 2026:

- Laser swords, laser blasters, rockets and rocket launchers are ancient machine-technology item families.
- Some machine technology has been reverse engineered into a crude Iron Age amalgamation of robotics, rocket science and advanced engineering. This establishes a present-day engineering tradition without specifying which of these weapon families has a working reproduction.
- Particular mechanisms, materials, power or propulsion supplies, compatible ammunition, operating performance, availability and recipes remain undefined. Ancient origin does not assign a crafting tier or imply the ability to reproduce ancient composites and other rare materials.

### Field Control And Electrical Protection

Author direction recorded October 14, 2026:

- Equipment families include magnetic field disruptors, inhibitors and enhancers, plus static-electricity field enhancers and disruptors. Their mechanisms, field strength, range, power supplies and targets remain open; the functional distinction between a magnetic inhibitor and disruptor is not yet defined.
- Arc ray guns, also described as lightning blasters, form an electrical-discharge weapon family separate from laser blasters.
- Insulating materials and insulating suits, gloves and boots exist for handling high-voltage electricity. Their composition, construction, operating limits and compatibility remain undefined; garment construction follows the established no-cloth material rules. Insulation and grounding remain distinct equipment purposes.
- Ancient, reverse-engineered or contemporary origins and crafting tiers have not been assigned to these families. Their existence does not establish particular combat effects, immunity, or enhancement/suppression of living peoples' abilities.

### Forging And Workshop Tooling

Author direction recorded October 14, 2026:

- Forging tools include hammers and anvils. Their construction, materials, crafting tiers and workshop requirements remain open.
- Contemporary steelmaking is fairly advanced. The Iron Age framing does not limit all metallurgy to crude iron. Steel is a recognized manufactured material; specific grades, compositions, production access, heat treatment, properties and crafting routes remain open. This capability does not establish reproduction of ancient composites or assign steel a numeric material tier.
- Supporting smithing tools and forge equipment in the item database remain brainstorming proposals. Heating and hot-work processes must respect the world's atmosphere/fire rules and material scarcity; ordinary combustion, wooden handles or free-water quenching are not established by this direction.

### Inner-City Transport

Author direction recorded October 14, 2026:

- The inner cities have carriages and cars; most of this transport machinery is bipedal.
- The exact relation of carriage and car designs to bipedal locomotion remains open. These names do not establish wheeled vehicles, draft animals or combustion engines. Power supplies, capacity, ownership, manufacturing and availability beyond the inner cities remain undefined.

### Metal Corrosion And Surface Treatment

Author direction recorded October 14, 2026:

- Advanced de-rusting materials exist. Their compositions, carriers, production routes and operating performance remain open.
- Rust removal, rust conversion and corrosion prevention are separate material functions in the item database; conversion treatments and protective finishes are supporting proposals. Mineral/faunal sources are possible directions, not accepted formulas. Ordinary humidity, free-water treatment baths and plant-derived reagents are not established by this direction; the world's actual corrosion conditions remain design work.

## Player Character

Booter is a professional desert survivor and metal scrapper.

- Booter's traversal gear includes a collapsible handheld wind sail and a four-wheel all-terrain dirtboard for sailing across large flat areas. Sail construction uses delicate insectoid wing material. Handling, steering, braking, carry/storage rules and movement tuning remain open; the equipment direction does not establish a working traversal implementation.
- Booter is not a chosen hero.
- Booter is highly trained, but still vulnerable.
- Movement should have momentum, commitment, and positioning cost.
- Combat should be slow, intentional, and punishing when poorly positioned.

## The Legger

The Legger (formerly BigARM) is a simple, physically present companion whose abilities are designed to work synergistically with Booter.

- The Legger is not a rover, vehicle, mobile habitat, home base, safe zone, or crafting platform. He is a finite, physically present mobile carrier; cargo access and transfer require his real simulated presence and never become remote storage.
- Booter and the Legger form one inseparable mechanical partnership even when they are physically separated in the world.
- The Legger may wander, choose local tasks, and operate autonomously.
- The Legger always has a true world position and must physically traverse the world to regroup with Booter.
- The Legger never snaps, teleports, or magically appears near Booter as a distance-recovery shortcut.
- The Legger exists in the world simulation, not as a menu abstraction.
- Further story changes to the Legger and his relationship with Booter remain open until the user defines them.

## World Structure

- The game is a continuous infinite open world.
- There are no discrete levels.
- There are no mission-based segments.
- Progression is about surviving deeper and managing longer distances from safety.

The world should naturally create:

- Canyon structures
- Traversable paths
- Depth layers
- Ruins
- Encounter zones

## Core Loop

1. Move through the world in coordination with the Legger, whether together or pursuing separate tasks.
2. Scout terrain and identify threats.
3. Salvage scrap, iron, and biological resources.
4. Regroup and combine Booter's and the Legger's complementary capabilities.
5. Craft tools, weapons, and upgrades.
6. Trade in settlements and outposts.
7. Plan and execute hunts.
8. Survive the consequences.

## Hunts

Hunts are large systemic encounters embedded in the world, not isolated boss arenas.

Targets may include:

- Ancient war machines
- Massive insectoid organisms

Hunts require scouting, preparation, environmental positioning, and risk evaluation.

## Tone

- Bleak, barren, and dead
- Quietly beautiful in its emptiness
- Minimal UI
- Environmental readability
- Sound and motion cues
- Systems over spectacle

The player does not observe the world. They operate within its systems.
