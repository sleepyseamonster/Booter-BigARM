# Saved Workbench cliff source as a generated module: rejected visual trial

2026-09-29. This is an isolated mirror experiment, not a production asset change or an accepted canyon treatment. The live Unity checkout's dirty World Creator files, production scene, source prefab, and saved profile were not edited.

The saved `Assets/_Project/Art/Environment/Rocks/Source/CliffWallSampleReference.prefab` has 18 editable Rock Workbench rocks, but its 36 `MeshFilter.m_Mesh` references are empty. Instantiating it directly renders nothing. [The temporary baker](WorkbenchWallModuleBakerTrial.cs.txt) reconstructed all 18 rocks from their saved volume nodes with `TopDown3DRockWorkbenchMesher`, combined the source transforms, and saved three temporary mesh LODs. The source prefab SHA-256 was `4de714183f13d9dcb57486ef293f2cc8b0e6ed8cf6d87b20e35d2729db8d6957`; the baker reported a Unity dependency hash of `2cb6b352b6fffec5ebad4fd6bebf8af8`. LOD0/1/2 contained 53,096 / 17,432 / 6,456 triangles respectively. This preserves the saved Workbench geometry as the source; it does not substitute generic rock families.

[The trial decorator](WorkbenchWallModuleDecoratorTrial.cs.txt) used seeded absolute cliff-section IDs, chunk-owned objects, three render LODs, and an LOD2 mesh collider. It screened a 4× scaled module's 6.3 m radius footprint against canyon-floor semantic, site/approach reservations, both proof-agent routes and slope, spawn clearance, and approved formation envelopes. The original 7.5× scale found no valid floor site in the visible 49 chunks. At 4× with a bounded 8–60 m search from the section toe, four sites were found. The other candidates failed because the sampled footprint reached steep ground, a route, or outside the floor. These screens must not be removed to increase rock count.

[The focused NUnit result](GeneratedWorkbenchModuleDeterminism.xml) passed 1/1: 49 chunks generated in normal and reverse order and after origin rebase produced the same collider IDs and quantized geometry. It also checked ray contact against each collider and verified that module collision used the LOD2 mesh. The three passes took 8.389, 8.353, and 8.324 seconds in Editor. This test's `faces=126` count includes four new module mesh colliders; `stones=131` is the existing box-collider count. The test did **not** establish module bottom contact across its full footprint, route traversal in Player, module-to-module spacing, unload/reload ring timing, save compatibility, or a rendered Player budget. [The exact fixture source](WorkbenchWallModuleFixtureTrial.cs.txt) is retained to make those limits auditable.

The [fixed game-camera view](GeneratedWorkbenchModuleGameCamera.png) at the first placed site shows the source composite as a tall, dark isolated pillar on the canyon floor. The [wider diagnostic](GeneratedWorkbenchModuleOverview.png) does not read as a continuous layered cliff either. **Visual gate failed.** Do not copy this runtime candidate or baked meshes into the production resource path. The next candidate needs a deliberately wall-shaped Workbench formation whose silhouette and material are designed with the terrain strata, plus terrain-contact and clearance proof before performance or cutover work.
