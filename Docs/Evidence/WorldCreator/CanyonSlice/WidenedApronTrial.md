# Widened floor and approved Workbench formation trial

Date: 2026-09-29. This is an isolated Unity mirror experiment, not a production cutover or visual acceptance. The live, dirty Unity checkout and source Rock Workbench assets were not changed. Seed `24681357`, canonical canyon segment `d1c85c86013ce2b97008b5c689c0d111`, Unity `6000.4.0f1`, and proof-only canyon-enabled profile match the parent [receipt](README.md).

## Why this trial exists

The earlier 49-chunk right-wall fixture had 212 fitted face spans, 37 individual `CliffStone_A-E` buttresses, and 149 source-linked talus rocks, but the face still appeared as giant slabs. These individual stone recipes are not the approved multi-rock Workbench formations. The existing canonical `WorldRockFormationPlanner` and `TopDown3DGeologicalRockAdapter` already route reservations through the approved `AuthoredFormationCatalog.asset`; the temporary fixed-render harness had omitted that production path. Adding the approved formation path to the proof render exposed the actual baseline: 29 parent reservations, six realized formations, all `HandbuiltSpire`, with 116 member rocks in five of 49 chunks. The spires appeared at a rim view, but did not repair the flat wall or the empty floor in the game-camera view.

The approved `MixedPileScatter` baked stage spans about 11.7 by 14.5 m at the current 1.85 game-world scale; its rotated half-diagonal footprint is about 9.3 m. `HandbuiltSpire` spans about 9.5 by 6.4 m. Five of seven baseline `MixedPileScatter` reservations were on 52-66 degree slopes; two gentle canyon-floor reservations failed protected route clearance. A center-only placement change would violate the full footprint screen.

## Mirror candidate

The [query trial patch](WidenedApronQueryTrial.patch) raises the lower shelf ratio by 0.20 and the middle shelf ratio by 0.10 for canyon sections at least 150 m wide. It does not move the centerline or directly lower its height. The [catalog trial patch](CanyonGoalTemplateTrial.patch) selects the first approved `FixedCluster` template for a `TalusFan` goal sampled on `CanyonFloor`; other goals retain the existing stable template choice. Both patches are relative to the preceding isolated gully candidate, not the live checkout. They are review artifacts and must not be applied blindly. The source fingerprint still reports `96ef0728256ce0e5c5524173e588aa7f` despite topology changes, so it cannot identify this variant; topology/landform and decoration/save compatibility require a deliberate version decision.

On a 4 m grid over absolute x=420..540, z=310..430, the baseline had 248 floor samples, 94 gentle unreserved centers, and no fully gentle, unreserved 5 m or 9 m footprints. The wider candidate had 551 floor samples, 391 gentle unreserved centers, 231 complete 5 m footprints, and 108 complete 9 m footprints ([space result](WidenedApronSpaceResults.xml)). This is a geometrical screening result, not permission to occupy every cell.

With the goal-aware template choice, the same 49 chunks realized six approved formations: two `MixedPileScatter` and four `HandbuiltSpire`, 118 member rocks in five chunks ([formation result](WidenedApronFormationResults.xml)). The floor scatter anchors are near absolute `(484.07,332.56)` and `(486.89,362.82)`. The chosen Workbench prefabs and baked stages were not edited. The [footprint result](WorkbenchFormationFootprints.xml) records the approved stage dimensions.

## Determinism and physical proof

The [three passing mirror checks](WidenedApronPhysicalResults.xml) built 122 fitted, colliding cliff spans, 29 individual Workbench buttresses, and 102 source-linked talus stones in the 49 right-wall chunks. Normal, reversed, and locally rebased builds matched generated IDs and face geometry. Six approved formations plus all 118 member rocks matched identity, template GUID, and centimeter-quantized absolute placement across normal, reload, and rebase builds. In the near rendered triangle screen, 43,920 fitted-face vertices protruded at most 0.300 m; 14,640 visual and the same collision triangle centroids protruded at most 0.998 m, with none over 1 m. Talus center gap was at most 0.22 m. These samples do not bound every surface point or prove hands-on passage. The established waypoint `(488.74,342.39)` remained query-walkable. The wider floor removed both nearby buttresses from the nine-chunk waypoint check, changing that local silhouette.

## Player build and streaming boundary

A macOS Development Player built successfully from this mirror candidate with the production build validator. The rendered background Player then stopped advancing immediately after the benchmark began; a process sample placed its main thread in `GfxDeviceMetal::WaitForLastPresentationAndGetTimestamp`. It was terminated after more than two minutes without a waypoint. Its initial telemetry is **not** a performance result. A second, headless Player run of the same binary completed the five-point automated streaming route ([filtered log](WidenedApronHeadlessPlayer.log)): exact requested absolute horizontal positions at stops 0, 2, and 4; chunk centers `23/25/27/25/23`; nine near terrain colliders; 49/49 adaptive far tiles by stop 1; and zero pending terrain/decorations at settled stops 1-4. This verifies background streaming/lifecycle convergence under the candidate, not rendered FPS, GPU cost, camera appearance, or hands-on traversal. The headless telemetry's high FPS and zero GPU time must not be used as a target-machine budget pass. The rendered Player blockage and the missing same-center cost comparison leave the performance gate open.

## Visual gate

The [fixed near overview](WidenedApronNearDiagnostic.png) has a broader dark floor and approved formation clusters, yet its pale walls still read as repeated flat slabs. The [fixed game-camera diagnostic](WidenedApronGameDiagnostic.png) mostly sees dark ground and a partial cluster, not the reference's layered mesa vista. The user's reference remains the visual target; this variant fails that gate. The attempted upper-wall outcrop trial was also rejected: 24-28 isolated source stones looked like specks on the slab and did not improve the 25 m game camera.

## Open decision before production

The next geometry pass must produce irregular **parent-scale** cliff/mesa mass and a readable layer rhythm across near, middle, and far representations. The approved Workbench formations can accent the rim, shoulder, and debris apron, but their 10-15 m footprint cannot supply a 100 m landform. Keep route and site reservations and full footprint screening intact. Compare the same canyon and camera pose with and without the new mass, then repeat collision, stream reload/rebase, exact-source provenance, and Player cost checks. Do not change the user's first-area canyon policy or saved-world authority as a side effect.
