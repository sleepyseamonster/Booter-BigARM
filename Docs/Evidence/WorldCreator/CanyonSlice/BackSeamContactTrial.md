# Buried back-seam contact trial

2026-09-29. Isolated Unity mirror work on the same generated canyon, seed `24681357`, with the terrain-matched fitted-face material from the [preceding trial](TerrainMatchedFaceTrial.md). No production asset, scene, shader, or dirty World Creator file changed. The [exact trial builder](BackSeamContactBuilderTrial.cs.txt) and [small patch against the preceding mirror builder](BackSeamContactBuilderTrial.patch) preserve the candidate; this is not a production cutover patch because the mirror contains other unaccepted canyon work.

## Geometry finding and change

The original contact probe found face vertices at most 0.300 m above the rendered near terrain, yet triangle interiors reached 0.992 m. Triangle-index diagnostics located the worst gaps in end caps and the buried back seam, including side 11 of the 12-point cross section. Broad adaptive splitting of every offending triangle nearly tripled sampled triangles from 2,520 to 6,950 while leaving a 0.963 m maximum ([rejected XML](BackSeamBroadSplitRejected.xml)). Lowering only end-cap centers reduced cap exposure but left a 0.977 m back-seam peak ([cap-only XML](BackSeamBuriedCapOnly.xml)).

The retained mirror candidate places the buried cap centers at least 2 m below the rendered near terrain, adds one buried center to each back-seam triangle, and limits the remaining ring vertices to 0.150 m above that terrain. The [final contact result](BackSeamFinalContact.xml) sampled 28 faces, 11,424 vertices, and 3,808 triangle interiors. Its maximum positive contact difference was 0.150 m at vertices and 0.394 m at both visual and collider triangle interiors; triangle-interior p95 was 0.239 m. No sampled positive protrusion exceeded 1 m. Deliberate burial put 1,563 sampled vertices more than 1 m below terrain. This is a sampled surface screen, not a proof of continuous whole-triangle intersection or physical traversal.

The [builder compatibility result](BackSeamBuilderCompatibility.xml) passed both original endpoint/LOD and shared-endpoint tests; those do not invoke the new terrain-fit callback. A new [fitted-endpoint test](BackSeamFittedEndpoints.xml), retained in the [fixture source](BackSeamProofTests.cs.txt), passed 1/1 with that callback and checks the complete shared end ring and cap between adjacent faces. The [49-chunk deterministic result](BackSeamFinalDeterminism.xml) passed normal, reversed, and rebased builds with identical IDs and mesh hashes: 248 fitted faces, 131 source-linked stones, 102 talus objects, and 29 buttresses. Talus center contact remained at most 0.22 m. The earlier material-matched candidate had 122 fitted faces in the same 49 chunks. Lowering buried geometry also lets more faces pass the existing whole-mesh terrain-contact screen; this growth is a material cost, not just a test-count difference.

The [fixed overview](BackSeamFinalOverview.png) has continuous terrain-colored ledges and no dark fitted-face bars, but the walls still read as smooth, oversized terraces with a weak talus apron. The overview uses the temporary render harness lighting. The [full-content Player third-stop frame](BackSeamFinalPlayerStop2.png) remains a dark, narrow floor-and-shadow view rather than the user's layered mesa landscape.

## Player route and cost boundary

The [macOS Development Player build](BackSeamFinalBuildResult.log) succeeded. Its [rendered 1034 × 546 full-content route](BackSeamFinalPlayerTelemetry.log) completed the same five requested absolute waypoints and center chunks `23/25/27/25/23`, retained nine terrain colliders at settles, and reached 49/49 decorated chunks and 49/49 adaptive far tiles. At settled stop three, the preceding mirror candidate and this build both had 240 loaded chunks, 49 decorated chunks, and 49/49 far tiles. The preceding candidate recorded 1,468 renderers, 358 colliders, and 227 decoration meshes; this build recorded 1,669, 458, and 427 respectively. Both single-run 5-second windows had roughly 8.3 ms average frame time and 9.2 ms p99 under a 120 FPS cap. This is a resource-count regression without a demonstrated settled frame-time regression. Arrival windows still vary, and a target-machine budget and frame/collider-cooking attribution remain open. The driver teleports and settles at each waypoint; it does not establish continuous Booter or BigARM traversal.

## Decision

Retain this as a mirror-only contact improvement. It improves sampled contact and keeps deterministic chunk identity, while the visual target remains failed and the number of fitted meshes/colliders grows. Before production promotion, either reduce the accepted face cost without losing useful layered mass or prove that cost within an agreed Player budget; fix the terrain-scale silhouette and camera view; then complete collision and hands-on traversal acceptance and decide topology/material/save versions. Keep the first-area no-deep-canyon policy intact.
