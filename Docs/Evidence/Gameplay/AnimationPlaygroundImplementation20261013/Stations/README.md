No unrelated body is discovered, moved or deleted. Unqualified legacy automatic climbing/vaulting is blocked in the lab; production defaults retain their prior traversal behavior.

The first proposed northeast rectangular layout could not fit the initial relief limit. A read-only native survey found even the gentlest sampled 28x24m footprints had over 2.3m of relief. The accepted candidate layout moves the same grid southwest, using flat decks with bounded support legs, perimeter guards and 3m-wide approach ramps selected from the higher native edge. [layout.json](./layout.json) records exact coordinates, sampled ground limits, ramp direction/rise/length and rejected proposals. Footprints remain 28x24m at 40m spacing. Ramp paths sample their full width and retain a 4–10m length and maximum 14-degree slope. No terrain is sculpted or regenerated.
