## Author test route

1. Start Play Mode, open F9 (or gamepad Select), and choose **Reset to gait deck**.
2. On AP02, sweep ordinary stick deflection through the accepted forward family and hold RB for sprint. Hold LT from a stop for slow left/right strafing; look-ahead remains active. Release/rehold LT after an action to rearm facing lock.
3. Release LT and reverse the stick on the flat turning circle: braking should lead into a full pivot. Try ordinary moving curves and B cancellation, returning the stick to neutral after cancelling.
4. Use AP02's existing **Mild ramp** on the right, approximately 7.1 degrees, uphill/downhill and across it. Try the same slow lateral travel. Seams, edges and steeper/uneven ground may visibly use baseline presentation.
5. Press A from standstill, slow lateral travel, each forward gait and RB sprint. Test neutral air momentum and countersteering. AP04 retains its steps/drop platforms and landing zone; use them for ledge falls, landing, optional selected roll recovery and reset. Check low-ceiling/edge behavior using the existing fixtures.

The combined review is ready for this author-owned test. Motion-quality observations above remain experimental limits, not completed physical or visual acceptance.
