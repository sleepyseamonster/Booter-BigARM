# Game references for painterly stylized realism

Sable, October 15, 2026. Research goal requested by the author: brainstorm additional games, then research how their art could inform a blend of Arcane, No Rest for the Wicked, and realism.

**Best additional match: Dishonored 2.** Its documented combination of hand-painted textures, controlled detail, designed shapes, and more realistic material rendering closely addresses the brief. Kena: Bridge of Spirits is the strongest complementary lighting reference; GreedFall supplies a useful classical-painting and realism comparison. Other candidates contribute narrower lessons rather than complete replacement styles.

The accepted direction remains **B's ground and a restrained B–C balance for rocks and props**, retaining C's broader painted accents. Rankings and applications below are Sable's recommendations, not new approvals. Arcane and No Rest for the Wicked remain the controlling inspirations; this expands the [existing reference library](RESOURCE_CATALOGUE.md), [realism research](REALISM_REFERENCES.md), and [stylized research](STYLIZED_REFERENCES.md).

## Candidate list and research outcome

The brainstorm selected these ten games to cover painting, believable lighting/materials, simplification, and tactile form. Dishonored 2 was already in the library and receives a deeper material study here; the other nine extend it.

| Game | Reference role after research | Fit to the requested blend | Main restraint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dishonored 2 | Painted materials and believable industrial design | Closest overall bridge | Keep deliberate distortion appropriate to our grounded survivor |
| Kena: Bridge of Spirits | Stylized forms supported by realistic lighting | Strong lighting complement | Its lushness and rounded character treatment are separate choices |
| GreedFall, original game | Classical-painting palette and realistic landscape depth | Strong realism complement | Painting inspiration does not prove hand-painted asset textures |
| Enshrouded | Cohesive survival-world dressing and material families | Useful world-building comparison | Do not adopt its voxel terrain or assume its assets are hand-painted |
| Ashen | Quiet surfaces, large masses, and atmospheric weight | Useful simplification reference | Its faceless characters and sparse finish go farther than our brief |
| Sea of Thieves | Economical painted ground and rock design | Useful surface-design reference | Temper the deliberately wonky shapes and playful proportions |
| Darksiders II | Monumental forms, sculpted architecture, and modular cohesion | Useful form reference | Heroic scale and ornament can overwhelm vulnerability |
| Lost in Random | Tactile imperfection and authored material consistency | Useful handcrafted reference | Clay-miniature logic and fairytale distortion are a distinct aesthetic |
| Atlas Fallen | Desert scale, buried civilization, and architecture | Useful setting and realism reference | Its power fantasy is outside our survival tone |
| Darkest Dungeon II | Preserving painted marks when moving into 3D | Useful process and contrast boundary | Heavy ink and extensive black masses exceed our realism balance |

## Closest matches

### Dishonored 2

Environment artist Yannick Gombart explains that the team retained manual painting, shared brushes, and a material library while the sequel's physically based rendering and increased texel density made materials feel more realistic. He identifies excessive small detail in color and normal maps as a threat to stylization and emphasizes medium-scale detail. This is unusually direct evidence for our intended balance. [Gombart's production interview](https://80.lv/articles/asset-material-production-in-dishonored-2).

**Application:** concentrate rock accents and prop wear into readable groups, then let roughness and light establish material identity. Keep enough grain to identify stone without covering every plane equally. This is a closer guide to our B–C balance than adding a global painterly filter.

Mitton's Karnaca interview also describes research into real places and lighting, with city history informing visible design. That supports designing machinery and settlements around use, exposure, and history. [Art director interview](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2016/10/03/crafting-dishonored-2s-karnaca-los-angeles-meets-skull-island).

The wall-material showcase contains a street image labelled **Dishonored**, with worn greenish masonry, large trim forms, pipes, and a warm doorway. It supports a broad franchise comparison; it is not labelled as a Dishonored 2 capture. [Material showcase](https://80.lv/articles/the-wall-materials-of-dishonored-2).

### Kena Bridge of Spirits

Mike Grier explicitly describes stylized characters and environments paired with realistic lighting. He also explains the importance of lighting each space and using common world assets across gameplay and cinematics. [Ember Lab interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/the-magic-of-creating-kena-bridge-of-spirits?lang=en-US).

The inspected village-running image shows a relatively open path framed by dense buildings and foliage, with softer illumination into the distance. My useful takeaway is the relationship between simplified forms, contact, atmosphere, and light. It does not establish the surface-authoring method of every rock or prop.

**Application:** retain our painted albedo but test believable illumination and material separation around it. Borrow the clarity of the path and the grounding of objects; translate those principles into barren terrain. Julian Vermeulen is a useful credited environment-art entry point. [Studio profile](https://emberlab.com/2021/02/16/get-to-know-julian-vermeulen-our-lead-environment-level-artist/).

### GreedFall

Spiders' original announcement describes a Baroque visual foundation, warm muted colors, and dark undertones. It is a pre-release statement of intent, not evidence that every announced choice shipped unchanged. [Jehanne Rousseau's introduction](https://blog.playstation.com/2017/02/09/greedfall-is-a-fantasy-rpg-with-combat-diplomacy-and-deception/).

Frederic Chemin's original-game environment gallery supplies a finished-work comparison. Its opening landscape uses a pale sky, layered mountain silhouettes, a waterfall, trees, and foreground rock. The useful lesson is tonal depth and restrained color relationships; the vegetation, water, and sky do not transfer to our world. Chemin identifies work on lighting, level building, the day/night cycle, and skyboxes. [Natural Part 1 gallery](https://cheminfrederic.artstation.com/projects/3onyDJ).

**Application:** study how realistic terrain can sit inside a composed image without every surface demanding equal attention. Preserve our orange sky and accepted ground treatment. Use the original game distinctly from The Dying World sequel/prequel material.

## Focused supporting references

### Enshrouded

Keen's world-design team describes hand-placing important location-defining elements and using reusable options for secondary props. It also describes shaping dramatic locations to communicate increasing difficulty. These are useful hierarchy lessons, separate from the game's voxel tools. [Official world-team AMA recap](https://enshrouded.com/es-ES/news/enshrouded-world-design-team-reddit-ama-recap).

The inspected official combat press image stages dark figures against blue light and a red sky. It supports a palette and silhouette comparison, not close analysis of ground or stone textures. **Application:** make landmarks and important props intentional focal points while keeping surrounding dressing subordinate. Its survival genre alone does not make its material finish our target. [Studio page and press imagery](https://www.keengames.com/enshrouded).

### Ashen

A44's Simon Dasan describes an iterated visual direction intended to feel beautiful yet mysterious, with faceless characters serving the game's relationship theme. [Developer interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/spotlights/a44-games-explains-how-they-designed-the-beautifully-haunting-world-of-ashen).

The official gallery shows a dark, desaturated fight with broad rock forms beside a warmer architectural scene. Large masses and restrained surface activity give both scenes presence. **Application:** use it to test how much detail our rocks can lose while retaining weight; preserve more material distinction and painted texture than this boundary example. Faceless characters are not part of the proposal. [Official gallery](https://www.ashen-game.com/).

### Sea of Thieves

Rare's art-direction article discusses an early beach image as a point where the game's visual style came together. The inspected image groups sand into broad patches, uses economical rock planes, and separates a distant formation through atmosphere. It is early development artwork, not a calibrated runtime material comparison. [Art of Adventure](https://www.seaofthieves.com/news/archive/a-closer-look-at-the-art-of-adventure).

**Application:** useful for B's quiet ground and readable broad rock marks. Keep physical fracture and construction more believable than its playful distortion. The beach, water, foliage, and bright tropical palette are reference content only.

### Darksiders II

Environment artist Robert Giampa describes modular team-built assets assembled from designer layouts through final polish. His `WB_01` screenshot shows monumental gates, repeated architectural masses, and a small character that conveys scale. The available preview supports composition and silhouette assessment more than fine texture analysis. [Artist's environment gallery](https://robertgiampa.com/darksiders-ii-environment-art/).

**Application:** borrow coherent shape families and the contrast between large structures and a small survivor. Use selective chipped planes and architectural repetition; keep ornament and oversized equipment restrained. This is a shape reference, not a reason to replace our Unity materials with a legacy rendering approach.

### Lost in Random

Art director Victor Becker describes clay studies, physical-media exploration, a deliberately limited material vocabulary, and iterating between thumbnails, blockouts, paintovers, and finished environments. The inspected Two Town alley preview shows crooked architecture and warm windows within cool surroundings; its small resolution limits texture inspection. [The Art of Random](https://www.ea.com/ea-play/news/art-of-random).

**Application:** useful for small handmade irregularities in props and a coherent family of authored marks. Keep our ceramic, stone, and metal distinguishable. Making all substances resemble a miniature sculpted in a few craft materials would be a new direction requiring separate review.

### Atlas Fallen

Art director James Lowe describes sand, buried ruins, and human settlements as central to the setting. This makes it relevant to subject matter, rather than evidence of an Arcane-like texture process. [Art director's introduction](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2022/08/23/upcoming-action-rpg-atlas-fallen-lets-you-turn-sand-into-coarse-rough-and-irritating-weapons/).

Constantin Copony's Spire gallery shows pale masonry, metallic accents, large openings, and clear architectural layering. He credits contributions spanning architecture, props, textures/shaders, modular sets, and trim sheets, alongside the wider team. [City of the Sun gallery](https://constantin-copony.artstation.com/projects/yDLGeR).

**Application:** compare material scale across immense ruins and small details. Use its buried-civilization reference without importing magical sand powers, its noon-like lighting, or its combat proportions.

### Darkest Dungeon II

Chris Bourassa describes hand-painting character textures with the original game's brush settings, combining shaders and real-time light, and building environment geometry to support initial 2D artwork. The inspected combat screenshot shows selective painted highlights and heavy dark contours separating figures from a dim background. [Bourassa's production interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-a-theme-of-a-road-trip-from-hell-would-shape-darkest-dungeon-ii).

**Application:** preserve the character of authored marks across a 3D pipeline and decide which edges deserve emphasis. Use this as the graphic end of the comparison: extensive black fill and comic contours could hide material and footing in our elevated camera. Its close staged battle view cannot establish our gameplay readability.

## Recommended reference mix

| Visual question | Lead reference | Complement | Proposed decision criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Painted surfaces with realism | Dishonored 2 | Arcane and the existing B/C study | Medium-scale accents remain readable while stone, ceramic, and metal respond differently to light |
| Believable lighting on designed forms | Kena | Diablo IV research | Objects feel grounded under both brighter sunset and deeper twilight without losing painted color |
| Ground restraint | Accepted B ground | Sea of Thieves and Ashen | Broad ground variation supports navigation and small usable props |
| Rock plane design | No Rest for the Wicked and accepted B–C balance | Dishonored 2 and the earlier Iron Alluvium study | Painted marks reinforce fracture and weight; seams and grain do not dominate |
| Ruins and machinery at scale | Atlas Fallen | Darksiders II | Large silhouettes communicate scale before ornament; supports and joints remain plausible |
| Palette and depth | GreedFall | Existing painting references | Warm world colors stay coherent while value and atmosphere separate distance |
| Authored imperfection | Arcane | Lost in Random | Small irregularities feel intentional without making all materials look like clay |
| Painted marks surviving 3D | Darkest Dungeon II | Dishonored 2 | Brush character survives at play distance without requiring heavy outlines everywhere |

These are reference roles, not percentages or a recipe to combine every game's signature feature. Start with **Dishonored 2 for surface balance, Kena for lighting, and GreedFall for tonal composition**. Use the remaining games to answer specific questions. The strongest proposal is believable form and material response with deliberately edited painted detail, rather than equal amounts of realism and exaggeration on every asset.

## Evidence and scope

Research covered all ten candidates through studio statements, contributor accounts, or direct developer interviews. Selected visuals were inspected for each, with Dishonored's inspected street image explicitly labelled as the original game. Darksiders II and Lost in Random previews limit fine-detail judgments. Enshrouded's selected image is promotional combat imagery. Full games, talks, and books were not reviewed end to end, and proprietary shaders were not reproduced.

The source links above form the reference shelf. Public images are for reference viewing, not a production asset license; no external media was copied into the repo. Recommendations respect the [world basis](../../../../WORLD_BASIS.md): barren canyons, no plant life or natural free water/rain, an orange sky, rust/iron colors, and a grounded survival tone. No new world lore is established.

This goal produces research and documentation only. No production assets, scenes, terrain, packages, materials, or source models changed. Deterministic world identity, terrain streaming, stable generated-object identity, placement constraints, and persisted runtime deltas are not applicable because no runtime content or behavior was created. Future asset studies or production implementation need their own bounded brief and the existing specialist handoffs.
