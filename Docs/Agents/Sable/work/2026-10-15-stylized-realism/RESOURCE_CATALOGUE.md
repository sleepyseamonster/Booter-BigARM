# Stylized realism reference and resource catalogue

Researched October 15, 2026. The priorities below are Sable's recommendations for this game's art research. **Start** entries most directly answer the author's requested direction; **Support** entries address a particular craft problem; **Boundary** entries help define how far to simplify or exaggerate.

Public artwork and studio examples are linked for reference. They are not a licensed production asset pack. Asset-library licenses are recorded separately. Access notes describe what was available during this research, not ownership or a promise of permanent availability.

## Suggested reading sequence

1. Read Moon's interview and Fortiche's visual philosophy for the main target.
2. Compare Durand's early Wayfinder account with the credited artist showcase.
3. Inspect Ori's official screenshots and the relevant GDC session for depth and focal hierarchy.
4. Read the Karnaca gallery for how environmental conditions can motivate design.
5. Study Sargent and the classical-lighting references before choosing surface noise or filters.
6. Use the practical tools only to answer the bounded questions in the [comparison study](COMPARISON_STUDY.md).

The catalogue contains 34 resources. It deliberately mixes finished imagery, production accounts, fundamentals, and usable source-material services so each solves a different part of the art problem.
