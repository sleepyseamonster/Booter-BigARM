# Rock Landmark Workbench

The Rock Landmark Workbench is the editor-only composition tier above the Rock Formation Workbench. It combines several editable formations into one large silhouette without flattening their formation or rock controls.

The authoring hierarchy is:

1. Editable source volumes build one `Rock Workbench`.
2. Several rocks build one `Rock Formation Workbench`.
3. Several formations build one `Rock Landmark Workbench`.

This is an evaluation and authoring system. It does not replace the absolute-space `WorldRockFormationPlanner` or change chunk ownership, streaming, persistence, or runtime realization.

## Create a generated landmark

Use:

`GameObject > Booter & BigARM > Top Down 3D > New Random Rock Landmark`

The first landmark type is `Spire Complex`. It creates only the two visually accepted formation families:

- Tall `Pile Of Rocks` formations establish the primary crown and secondary peaks.
- Medium pile and scattered formations form irregular shoulders.
- Broad, low piles and scattered fields create a rubble apron.
- Small scattered formations break the perimeter into isolated outliers.

`Width`, `Height`, and `Asymmetry` are the main controls. Formation count, role proportions, individual formation dimensions, placement falloff, and child seeds are derived automatically. `Generate New Landmark` creates a new seed. `Update Current Landmark With These Settings` preserves the current seed and reconstructs the child formations. Both operations replace generated children as one Undoable authoring action.

The advanced `Approach Opening` control reserves a quieter outer sector without making the landmark one-sided. Every generated child formation receives a unique seed. The hierarchy names formations by `Crown`, `Shoulder`, `Apron`, or `Outlier` role so the result remains understandable and manually editable.

## Capture an authored landmark

To preserve an existing composition:

1. Select at least two `Rock Formation Workbench` roots, or objects beneath those roots.
2. Choose `GameObject > Booter & BigARM > Top Down 3D > Create Landmark From Selected Formations`.
3. The command creates one `Rock Landmark Workbench` at the selection center and reparents the formations while preserving their world transforms and child rocks.

Capture does not regenerate, rescale, or reposition the selected formations. It also leaves `Share Geological Field` disabled initially so an accepted material arrangement does not change merely because it was grouped. The shared field can be enabled afterward.

## Geological and terrain behavior

When `Share Geological Field` is enabled, child formations keep unique geometry seeds but use the landmark seed and origin for broad material patches and long fractures. This makes the entire landmark read as one geological event without repeating identical formation geometry.

When `Conform To Terrain` is enabled inside the Landscape Authoring Sandbox, each generated formation root samples the production terrain at its center and four inset support points. A low support quantile seats broad piles into the ground instead of balancing them on the highest sample. Scattered formations retain their existing per-rock terrain seating after the formation root is placed.

## Editing and performance boundary

Child formations and their rocks remain normal workbenches. They can be moved, regenerated, resized, or edited after landmark generation. Pile formations retain their own fused geological shell; scattered formations retain separate rocks. The landmark does not attempt to fuse every source volume across the entire hierarchy into one live mesh because that would make iterative editing unnecessarily expensive.

Ground clutter, wind-shaped sand, final baking, runtime LODs, collider simplification, and procedural-world adoption are later slices. They should consume the landmark footprint and role plan rather than becoming independent placement systems.

## Deterministic hierarchy

The authoring seed path is:

`landmark seed -> formation role/index -> unique formation seed -> rock seed -> source-volume seed`

Future runtime adoption must derive equivalent identities from the canonical world seed, generation version, absolute landmark identity, and owner address. A formation spanning several realization chunks must remain one logical landmark owned by its world reservation rather than being independently regenerated by each chunk.
