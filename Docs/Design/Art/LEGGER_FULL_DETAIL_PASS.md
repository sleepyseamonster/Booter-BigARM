# Legger whole-model detail pass

October 16, 2026. The author requested continued visual auditing and refinement of the complete model, including panel/plating angles, mounting, stirrups, foot pads and controls, with **no additional geometry** and small intricacy carried by textures. This extends the [original-led rider rebuild](LEGGER_RIDER_REBUILD.md) in the same organized native scene. The original dense Legger remains the design reference; the fitted saddle, grip contacts, saved scale and accepted aligned three-section joint targets remain constraints.

## Audit and response

| Inspected area | Finding and next-pass response |
| --- | --- |
| Whole silhouette and proportions | Keep the accepted three-section leg chain, broad support feet, compact saddle and four fitted cases. Inspect matching front, rear, side, top and underside views against the retained original. No global rescale or rest-pose change. |
| Leg plating | Broad uninterrupted rounded covers read as inflated shells. Flatten spans between existing section anchors into firmer planes, preserving the closed inner/outer construction. Add long access-cover boundaries, sparse shallow vent slots, slotted retainers and small witness marks in the maps. |
| Front and waist plating | Reduce the swollen crown into a broad center plane with angled shoulders, using existing vertices. Add panel breaks and recessed access details without separate fittings. Keep the descended rider waist and front cowling outline. |
| Frame, chassis and case mounting | Retain the cast diagonals, yokes, hip clevises, saddle supports and fitted case racks. Bare-frame oblique/side and underside views expose the existing load paths and service routing. Add restrained surface breaks and witness marks; preserve the case bodies, lids, straps and rack geometry. Static appearance does not certify engineering strength or every assembled intersection. |
| Drive and cooling | Add bounded louver/slot relief and small service marks to the existing housings. Keep their silhouette and actual major fins. These are surface slots, not new through-holes. |
| Saddle and rider interfaces | The previous original-led compact crown, pocket, rear rise, trim and stitch treatment remain useful. Keep both saddle meshes and their packed maps exact. Keep grip, lever, stirrup and tread geometry in the fitted position. |
| Stirrup and machine feet | Keep the open loops, narrow load tread, chassis brace and broad robot support feet. Add fine contact texture to the existing rubber surfaces. Preserve ground contacts and support height. |
| Handlebars and controls | Preserve the raised swept bar, grip axes and lever placement. Add crossed grip knurl and a restrained status graphic through the maps. Close inspection found the display buried in its housing; move only its existing face outward along its rider-facing normal. Refine the offset from an 18 mm trial to 11 mm so its back stays embedded in the housing. No control objects or faces are added. |

The new surface details are native analytic material work baked into the existing module UVs. They are not a projection of the generated model's lumps, purchased textures, or a new lore/material proposal. The approved ivory, dark metal, brown leather and restrained wear remain. Small retainers are texture relief rather than separate nuts or bolts.

## Budget and verification contract

The hard ceiling is the preceding saved model: **76 active major meshes, 45,762 control quads and 146,264 evaluated triangles**. Count modifiers as well as the controls. Preserve control connectivity, existing atlas coordinates, all active transforms, eleven independent module roots and one complete assembled scene/view layer. Only the selected existing vertex positions change. All earlier mesh objects and packed image bytes must remain available in hidden references and an independently hashed recovery file.

Check closed/nondegenerate quad controls, preserved topology/UVs/transforms, mirrored edited pairs, unchanged joint pivots and unchanged rider-contact geometry. Validate packed maps, color spaces, sampled UV coverage and normal lengths. Inspect native-source materials, the baked candidate, all-around views and detailed mounting/control/foot views. Independently reopen the saved native source and compare its appearance with the candidate before closeout.
