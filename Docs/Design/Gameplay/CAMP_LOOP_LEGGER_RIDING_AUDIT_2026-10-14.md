# Camp loop Legger riding audit

October 14, 2026. Scope: [CampLoopTesting](../../../Assets/_Project/Scenes/Reference/CampLoopTesting.unity), the existing companion Legger, and mounting, riding and difficult-terrain control. The author requested the audit as the current goal. Riding implementation is the intended next feature; this audit changes no gameplay.

The existing Legger provides a useful physical companion foundation, but mounting and riding are not implemented. His current follower is faster than Booter in this lab, yet has stricter terrain limits and controls designed for autonomous following. A robust riding slice needs one movement authority, validated mount/dismount transitions, direct analog control, and a traversal profile that demonstrably improves mobility.

The author's current riding direction supersedes older statements that the Legger is not implicitly rideable. It preserves his real world position, physical regrouping and finite cargo. The separate [enemy audit](CAMP_LOOP_LEGGER_ENEMY_AUDIT_2026-10-14.md) and Lager enemy proposal do not establish a companion riding implementation. The [companion standard](BIGARM_COMPANION_STANDARD.md) still governs physical continuity.

## Recommended controls and behavior

Keep camera-relative left-stick/WASD movement, right-stick/mouse orbit and existing look-ahead behavior. Use analog magnitude for walking through camp and maneuvering near obstacles; retain the current sprint button as the mounted fast gait. Release should brake predictably, reversal should pass through a controlled deceleration, and low-speed steering should stay responsive. This direct movement model fits the existing top-down controls; steering should not require vehicle-style throttle management.

Recommend contextual tap Interact to mount a nearby, available, stopped Legger and to request dismount when already riding. Retain cargo as a separate action. Keyboard E already has tap interaction versus hold cargo semantics; gamepad West is Interact and gamepad cargo shortcut is deliberately disabled in the router. Add a live mount interaction target with clear prompt and range/visibility checks, rather than having proximity silently consume every Interact. A selected world target should retain its interaction; show the mount prompt when the Legger is selected. Capture target identity at press time, validate again at completion, and never turn a rejected mount into a gather or cargo action.

On mount request, reserve the nearby Legger and let him brake physically; validate the approach, seat and combined clearance. Booter mounts locally through a checked transition, not a distant summon. Suspend follow/call-over intent while ridden. On completion, fresh steering can flow into riding without requiring an unnecessary stick release; consume the initiating Interact edge so holding it cannot immediately dismount. Menus, device loss, focus loss and lab reset require their existing neutral/rearm protections and safe braking. Inventory/radial suppression must stop the ridden actor as well as Booter; a paused system menu must retain the relationship without continuing a transition on unscaled time.

Reject mounting during gathering, placement, camp approach/rest/sleep, waypoint travel, crouch/prone ownership, station action, preview, climb, vault or reset. While riding, ground harvesting, canister placement and camp entry require a dismount; use feedback rather than letting Booter execute them from the seat. Keep cargo access physically local, and do not give a menu authority to detach or relocate either actor. Jump/crouch/attack inputs must be explicitly suppressed or assigned a qualified riding action; do not let the lab's earlier animation command resolution steal a dismount or trigger an on-foot action.

For dismount, brake first and evaluate authored side exits in stable order, with live support, standing clearance, full exit sweep and terrain checks. Revalidate after the animation before handing movement back to Booter. Do not accept a clear endpoint reached through a blocked path. After release, clear stale follow trails/call intent and resume following from the Legger's actual position without a lurch. Repeated presses, interruption and object disable/unload must converge on one valid relationship, without duplicate ownership, invisible colliders or a free-falling rider.

Start speed studies from the existing 5.8/8.4 m/s values, then judge whether their benefit survives load and turning. Heavy-load catch-up is 6.888 m/s before slowdown/turn factors, about 1.81 times this lab's 3.8 m/s sprint, but below production's saved 7.4 m/s sprint. That comparison is tuning arithmetic, not a measured ride. Set the desired production advantage separately; avoid claiming the lab alone proves it. Rider mass, stamina, jump ability and cargo balance penalties remain design choices; none is needed as an invented punishment to establish riding.

## Implementation sequence and proof

1. **Relationship and transitions.** Add a small companion riding coordinator, one explicit locomotion owner and an authored riding profile in `BooterBigArm.TopDown3D.Runtime`. Use states such as OnFoot, Mounting, Mounted and Dismounting, with suspension/interruption handled explicitly. Keep the existing follower as the autonomous intent producer; separate reusable physical movement checks from its route-follow decision. Prove ownership exclusivity, local mounting, repeated requests, failed entry rollback and blocked exits before increasing speed.
2. **Direct control and terrain.** Add camera-relative analog intent, acceleration, braking and turning with one physics-step writer. Use swept body/rider clearance, multiple support samples and explicit slope/step/drop rules. Test whether geometry permits a safe slide along an obstacle before cancelling forward movement. Prove that direct steering does not wait for the follower's 0.75 s route cycle, and blocked motion neither tunnels nor invents displacement.
3. **Camp integration and presentation.** Author a bounded riding corridor in CampLoopTesting with open ground, a slalom, narrow passages, stepped ground, slopes on both sides of thresholds, cliff edge, low headroom and obstructed exit sides. Integrate prompts, camera blend, rider pose, action arbitration and reset without rebuilding the whole scene or changing production. Preserve current dirty scene work. Keep native terrain unchanged.
4. **Acceptance and later production gate.** Run focused EditMode/structural checks for the implemented contracts. Hands-on testing remains with the author: analog creep, diagonal speed, fast reversal, camera orbit while steering, rough-ground stability, acceleration/braking, visible mount/dismount contact, cargo load differences and blocked-exit feedback. Promote only after both automated contracts and feel acceptance pass. Production persistence is a separate versioned slice, not an old generated-world save service installation.

Required automated coverage for implementation: exactly one movement writer; repeated mount/dismount inputs; failure at each transition phase; mount selection versus other targets and cargo hold; tap/hold and rebinding parity in the runtime lab asset; device/focus loss and menu return; cancelling with held input; every exit blocked; collider/body restoration; combined clearance during turns; thin obstacles at maximum speed; slope/step/drop thresholds; support loss; cargo change while mounted; reset and disable/re-enable; no companion recall relocation; and no checkpoint file writes from the lab. Do not create a gameplay smoke test for this audit.

Existing inspected tests cover local grid corner/barrier/step rejection, call-time non-relocation, locomotion math, smart traversal and camp scene structure. They do not establish mounted behavior. No Unity test execution, player build or hands-on riding claim accompanies this source audit.

## World and persistence contract

- **World identity:** riding changes actors and movement capability on fixed authored terrain; it does not alter a terrain seed, heightfield, generation version or world identity.
- **Streaming and unload/reload:** native terrain is always loaded, so terrain streaming is not applicable. Dressing preparation still matters: prepare collision over the combined actor envelope and stopping distance before advancing. Scene unload invalidates commands and releases ownership without creating a new companion beside Booter.
- **Stable identity:** retain `legger.primary` and the existing cargo owner. Reference the companion by stable identity; transient collider IDs and a seat transform are not saved identity. No generated riding actor is needed.
- **Authored constraints:** riding profile, support classes, seat/exit anchors, combined clearance, obstacle exclusions and permitted traversal are explicit authored data. Respect camp footprints and fixture boundaries. No terrain reshaping or automatic permission to climb every solid.
- **Persisted deltas:** the current lab has no durable riding delta. Its reset must clean transient relationship state. Future production saves need coordinated actor poses and riding relationship policy, with validated restore at the saved place and no teleport-to-Booter recovery.
