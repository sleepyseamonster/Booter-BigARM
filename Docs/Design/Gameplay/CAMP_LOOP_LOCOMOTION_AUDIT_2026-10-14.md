# Camp Loop walking, running and sprinting audit

Date: 2026-10-14. Scope: audit and reassessment only, following the author's report of odd animation during playtesting. The broader animation playground implementation is stopped while these three gaits take priority. No runtime, clip, binding, prefab, scene or input repair is included.

The current gait foundation needs repair before more interaction families are enabled. The accepted rig and calibrated profile are present in the live CampLoopTesting scene. The visible capsule placeholder is not an animation dependency. The separate physics capsule remains necessary, but changing it cannot correct cadence, stride matching or pose handoffs.

## Evidence and limits

Read-only CLI inspection found CampLoopTesting loaded, clean and outside Play Mode, with the expected CalibratedLocomotion and PlayerBindings assets. The driver uses the accepted original rig at scale 0.08989948. During lab startup, configured speeds are walking 1.4m/s, running 2.7m/s and sprinting 3.8m/s. Keyboard walking uses Left Alt; ordinary movement runs; sprint uses the existing sprint binding. Analog magnitude is preserved by the movement basis. These are design/controller speeds, not measurements of the clips' planted stride.

The [audit evidence](../../Evidence/Gameplay/LocomotionAudit20261014/README.md) seals current source/profile/clip hashes, twelve cadence evaluations, nine steady pose-blend envelopes with 2,169 samples, and three independent fresh graph samplers with 443 frames. All 21 existing focused locomotion/gait Edit Mode checks pass. They establish structural/math contracts rather than playtest acceptance.

The graph measurements use the bare accepted target on a flat reference floor and the actual driver mixer, contact mapping and weighted height correction. They do not replay the author's session, simulate physics, evaluate equipment fitting, establish performance or replace visual/feel acceptance. Foot drift is a proxy computed from foot-bone velocity, filtered by sole proximity and rearward movement, plus commanded forward speed; it includes ordinary foot rolling and is not a direct measurement of a fixed contact vertex.

With the driver's semantic phase mapping, estimates are 0.120, 1.870 and 2.913m/s respectively. | Running and sprinting need stride/contact calibration first. | P1 | Sprint's source contact intervals are remapped to four equal semantic intervals. The resulting native segment rates at 3.8m/s are 0.5, 1.4375, 1.0625 and 1.0 despite profile bounds of 0.9–1.1. The pure loops range from approximately 0.13mm to 5.0mm penetration. | Grounding must qualify the evaluated blended pose, including intermediate gait weights. Tested turn blends penetrate up to 42.4mm and substantially increase walking's drift estimate. | Establish the forward trio before adding directional poses. Requalify turn clips against actual rotation/trajectory and gait speed. |
| P2 | The standing gait clock reads CurrentPlanarVelocity from the published next commanded physics velocity. The lab's actual-displacement measurement is available but does not drive standing cadence. | Wall contact, braking, slopes and partial analog travel need explicit motion-versus-pose checks. This is a code-level concern, not an observed collision reproduction in this audit. |

The height/stride defects do not establish a bad rig or require a new Blender export. The gait loops are valid Humanoid clips and their references are hydrated. Start/stop source compatibility, contact timing and locomotion ownership are the immediate engineering seams.

The saved motor still contains historical 4.2/7.4m/s defaults; the lab overrides them in session Start. A failed bootstrap could therefore magnify the mismatch. The current inspection was outside Play Mode and found no captured locomotion bootstrap failure; verify actual startup speeds during the next author playtest rather than treating the defaults as proof of the reported failure.

## Revised priority plan

1. **Establish three clean forward loops.** In the isolated candidate, evaluate walking, running and sprinting individually at the intended speeds. Keep idle as a reference. Isolate legacy starts/stops/pivots and strafe blends in the qualification harness so their effects can be attributed. Preserve author assets, GUIDs, motor ownership and existing physical interaction recovery.
2. **Measure stride and choose the motion family.** Review the existing downloaded alternatives before acquiring anything else. Measure stance windows, foot displacement, limb amplitude, forward orientation and native cadence on the accepted rig. Keep intended movement speeds as design inputs. Select or author/retime compatible clips; do not arbitrarily slow the player to conceal sliding. Store derivatives locally with recipes and hashes.
3. **Repair the continuous clock and contact mapping.** Use an explicit stride-count convention. Make cadence continuous at the actual dominant-gait midpoints and nominal speeds. Check the derivative of semantic-to-source time, including asymmetric sprint intervals, against accepted per-clip rate bounds. Use actual motion/displacement where needed; stop cadence when movement is blocked. Preserve full limb motion and one animation output.
4. **Qualify gait blends and ground contact.** Test intermediate walk/run/sprint weights, transitions, partial input, braking, 30/60/120Hz clocks and pause/resume. Measure evaluated sole clearance rather than only weighted correction curves. Keep grounding support-aware and bounded; preserve airborne arcs. Do not reintroduce competing bone writers or unqualified IK that previously collapsed strides.
5. **Add matching start/stop and turns.** Use gait-compatible enter/exit poses and planted-foot handoffs. Give each transition enough physical time to finish or a qualified interruption path. Reassess the current 4.2m/s jog family and the 55% strafe steering mix. Add them only after the forward trio is sound.
6. **Author playtest gate.** First review the three gaits and speed changes on AP02's flat deck, then steering, starts/stops, obstruction and native slopes. Make walk/run/sprint controls and current readiness visible. Record the author's appearance/feel acceptance and remaining defects before broader interaction work resumes.

The next implementation slice should be locomotion only: source/stride calibration and a continuous clock, followed by blend grounding. Pickup, push, climbing, cover, mining, rest, combat and additional motion acquisition stay deferred. Existing enabled crouch/jump/slide recovery must remain regression-safe, but extending those families is outside this priority slice.

## Acceptance and missing proof

The current tests validate ordered contact arrays, bounded aggregate cadence and clip/profile completeness. They do not check dominant-role midpoint continuity, actual warped playback rates, stance drift, mixed-pose sole penetration, transition pose compatibility or real collision displacement. Add those meaningful fixtures during repair; do not merely reproduce the current implementation's formulas.

Proposed repair gates: finite and continuous source timing with no dominant-role cadence step; explicit accepted limits for planted-foot drift and retiming; flat-floor sole penetration no more than 1mm through accepted blends; matched planted-foot handoffs; no stationary contacts while blocked; and author acceptance for all three gaits and their transitions. Drift/rate budgets require motion review before final tuning. Offline numerical success remains separate from visual feel.

This remains an authored, always-loaded laboratory. No generated identities, terrain/chunk streaming, terrain generation or persisted world deltas are introduced. Existing scene/prop identities and session teardown remain relevant to later implementation; the audit itself changes no gameplay state or save schema.
