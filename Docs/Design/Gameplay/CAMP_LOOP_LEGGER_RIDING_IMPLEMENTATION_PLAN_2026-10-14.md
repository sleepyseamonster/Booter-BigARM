Mounting brakes the Legger and validates an authored local entry corridor. Ignore the selected mount's own collider only where the seat transfer deliberately requires it; surrounding obstacles remain blocking. The initial transfer is a checked local attachment, not a summon. Commit exactly once; failure releases the reservation and leaves Booter on foot with captured settings intact.

At commit capture Booter's body kinematic/gravity/interpolation/collision settings, collider state, presentation parent/local pose and relevant ownership. Clear velocities, acquire passive motor ownership, suppress normal collider/ground locomotion, activate the combined clearance profile and attach the placeholder presentation. Keep Booter's root/inventory identity. Do not parent a dynamic Rigidbody beneath the Legger or disable the whole player motor. No mounting clip or animation event is required.

Dismount first brakes. Threshold proposal: measured speed at most 0.15 m/s. Previously held steering is suppressed while braking; only a fresh movement command after neutral cancels the pending exit. Select indicated side then left/right/rear in stable authored order. Validate the full seat-to-exit corridor, downward transfer, Booter's standing capsule, slope and separation. Recheck immediately before commit. A clear endpoint behind a blocked route is invalid. If every exit fails, stay mounted, report No safe exit and restore steering; do not retry endlessly. Release at rest without inherited launch velocity. Hold Legger locally until Booter clears his body, then rebuild follow trail/route from actual poses.

Menu/inventory/radial suppress movement and brake while retaining attachment. System menu suspends entry/exit commits; current menu changes input mode without globally pausing physics. Focus/device loss clears pending commands and safe-stops; rearm before movement resumes. Jump, crouch, attack, camp and waypoint actions require dismount. Recall/call-over while mounted is a harmless Already riding rejection. Cargo remains local and uses existing policy; no free transfers or rider item.

On reset invalidate epochs and pending entry/exit before actor restoration. Release transient ride ownership at the last valid pose, then let the existing registry resolve Booter's safe return; keep the Legger at his real coordinate. If restoration fails, input stays blocked. Scene unload releases transient references; isolated component disable/destroy requires bounded local safe detach or Faulted input-blocked handling at the last valid pose. ## Riding motion profile and safety rules

Initial profile candidates: normal gait 6 m/s, fast gait 10 m/s, acceleration 16 m/s squared, braking/reversal 24 m/s squared, turn rate 500 degrees/s at low speed blending toward 360 at fast gait. Preserve analog magnitude through the existing movement basis; no additional deadzone. Reversals decelerate before acceleration into the opposite heading. Report actual achieved displacement/speed, separately from intent and blocked reason. Keep cargo's existing speed/acceleration factors; braking remains effective independently of load. Heavy fast gait computes to 8.2 m/s, above production's saved 7.4 m/s sprint; acceptance is still measured in this lab.

Traversal candidates: 55-degree continuous slope, step up at most 1 m, controlled step down at most 1.25 m. Step movement caps at 2.5 m/s, with full landing support and clearance. Validate finite ordered settings and hard query caps; Inspector tuning cannot disable safety. Without a valid profile, leave ride unavailable with feedback, not an unbounded fallback.

Resolve movement in this order: prepare collision corridor; verify starting support/overlap; compute desired velocity and facing; classify slope versus discrete ledge; resolve step path if eligible; sweep body and rider over translation and rotation; resolve at most two safe wall-slide contacts; verify final support; commit allowed pose. Terrain vetoes stop unsafe components immediately, not through an acceleration ramp. Candidate pose and root motion are never committed before validation.

Use center plus footprint support samples. Choose support near prior surface height within local bounds; do not sample from a high ray origin and switch floors. A useful initial support rule requires the center and at least three perimeter samples on legal support with bounded spread, plus forward-edge support during advance. Reject narrow perches, missing forward support and discontinuous landings. Confirm this rule on rough terrain in Batch 3; loosen only with evidence that it remains cliff-safe. Terrain and explicitly marked ride-support solids are allowed; wreck roofs, camp props and unqualified rocks remain obstacles.

Continuous incline speed is measured along the surface; step caps apply to discrete discontinuities rather than every grid-cell height delta. Step-up uses a checked lift/across/settle path, not a diagonal cut through the ledge face. Step-down verifies descent and landing. Stop before larger unsupported drops; no unrequested free-fall mechanic. Keep physics upright; optional restrained presentation lean does not change clearance.

Validate translation with full shape casts and initial/final overlaps. For yaw rotation, use conservative swept envelopes across bounded orientation increments; a fixed-orientation box cast alone is insufficient. Initial hard limits: at most eight motion substeps per tick, 0.15 m linear/corner-travel target per substep, two slide resolutions, five support samples per required support pass, and at most 256 physics queries per tick. Track usage before each query; insufficient budget reduces motion or reports unavailable. Saturated fixed buffers reject conservatively. These are ceilings, not targets; no allocating fallback or hidden work after exhaustion. Profile validation derives maximum supported speed/turn displacement from these ceilings at the live fixed timestep.

At approximately 0.02 s fixed timestep, 10 m/s advances 0.20 m per tick and constant 24 m/s squared braking needs about 2.08 m. Collision preparation must cover stopping distance, body/rider footprint, skin and readiness latency, independently of dressing visibility. Reuse current PopulationPreparation seam if bound; absence does not create a generator. Keep seat, rider and camera in one interpolated presentation frame, preserving orbit/look-ahead. Blend framing over a candidate 0.2 s; do not call Configure/SnapToTarget on ordinary mount changes. Extend obstruction exclusions to both actors and preserve current initial-overlap recovery.

## Audited integration contracts

### Physics ordering and lease lifetime

Booter's mounted-yield check is the first motor branch, before PopulationPreparation, animation motion, camp/waypoint and ordinary action constraints. It publishes a mounted snapshot without writing body velocity, gravity or position. The Legger checks body initialization and the active ride/reservation lease before follow-target routing, startup grounding or the autonomous population region. Ride eligibility requires startup grounding already complete. The ride branch computes its own combined preparation region; unavailable coverage stops motion but still processes bounded cancellation, lifetime checks and timeout bookkeeping. No autonomous trail/grid work runs during the lease.

The follower commits one resolved pose per tick. Derive passive rider and companion published position from that same resolved pose, not `body.position` read immediately after a queued MovePosition. Tests exercise player-before-follower and follower-before-player ordering. Presentation follows the interpolated seat without another physics write. Do not solve ordering by changing global script execution order settings.

Follower/player/controller disable notifications invalidate the lease synchronously. Controller Update may supervise liveness and feedback but cannot move either actor. A disabled follower cannot be the only clock capable of releasing the rider. No stale owner resumes autonomous follow before passive Booter is restored or safely held Faulted. Disabling an actor is distinct from scene destruction; do not attempt normal scene recovery against objects already unloading.

### Target selection and command arbitration

The current interaction scanner takes the first matching target component per collider, and the existing cargo-access component also implements the target interface. Adding a mount component last is not enough. In the camp ride installation, use the selected Legger collider for one mount target and exclude that actor's cargo target from tap selection while the riding binding is active. Cargo hold/menu access remains governed by cargo state. Preserve uninstalled production target behavior and other target ranking. Test component order permutations and selection among Legger, salvage, Ironstone and stations.

Do not implement mounting by an extra InteractRequested subscriber racing the gather controller. The router consumes a riding command once at its existing update boundary; the player-action dispatch uses the captured target to route the on-foot mount case. Mounted dismount bypasses `IsBusy` only for its own riding command. Other commands retain their ordinary gates. Capture the current movement value at dismount request, suppress it during braking, and require neutral followed by a fresh above-deadzone input to cancel. Repeated Interact never toggles multiple transitions.

Mounted cargo may open only from a stable relationship outside entry/exit/step transitions and with no other work active. The action owner excludes only the ride-owned MotorOwned flag from cargo eligibility; all other busy states and existing `TopDown3DCargoAccess.CanAccess` range/visibility checks remain. Do not bypass transfer-time checks or increase cargo range because the seat is high. If authored seat geometry fails the existing access rule, show unavailable cargo and require dismount. Test transfer after modal return, companion disable and changed access.

### Step continuation and support contact

Lift/across/settle is a bounded movement subphase under Mounted, with an explicit path, landing and progress. Neutral input or a menu cannot simply drop its ownership mid-lift. When interrupted, finish a short revalidated safe settle or physically retreat along a revalidated entry path; if neither is legal, hold passive state and expose Faulted/reset recovery. Do not require ordinary center ground support during the deliberately airborne portion of a legal step, and do not waive clearance/landing validation there. No backward teleport to the step start.

Support classification does not exempt a whole ground collider from collision. The same TerrainCollider can contain the supported patch and a blocking bank. Permit only local contact within the profile's skin tolerance and compatible surface normal, validating the rest of the body/rider envelope. Root elevation must fit the upright body above the sampled support footprint; a center-height-plus-fixed-clearance rule is not sufficient on a steep slope. Add sloped body-clearance and terrain-bank tests alongside ray-normal threshold tests.

### Reset and scene data

Reset invalidation clears commands and freezes the relationship; it does not restore dynamic gravity at the elevated seat. Register a riding restoration participant before the existing Actor stage and connect to the actor-return commit. Resolve a verified local detach or an existing authored Booter safe-return target while the rider stays passive. Only after the player reset commits a supported standing pose may the riding owner restore body/collider/presentation settings and release the lease. Preserve Legger's pose and cargo. If the existing actor reset cannot commit, input remains blocked and the mount does not resume following. Registry Unregister is deferred until after a reset iteration; never mutate its participant list during reset.

The current `TryResetAnimationActor` rejects an action constraint owned by anyone except the lab posture owner, so a ride-owned constraint cannot simply be left in place and passed to the existing reset method. Add an explicit reset-only handoff verified against the existing lab reset owner, riding owner/epoch and LaboratoryResetBlocked. Refactor the actor-return commit into a shared checked kernel if needed; keep the mounted early-yield guard active while it transfers the constraint and commits the supported return. On failure retain passive ride ownership. Never relax the general owner check or release gravity before this commit. Add `AnimationPlayground/AnimationPlaygroundActionCoordinator.cs` and `TopDown3DPlayerMotor.AnimationPlayground.cs` to the Batch 5 source boundary for that narrow reset integration, with regression tests rejecting unrelated reset callers.

Proposed scene fields: explicit Booter motor, Legger follower, input, camera and lab session references; profile asset; seat frame; left/right and optional rear entry/exit frames. Use a 2.4 m initial mount reach, standing capsule clearance and authored corridor limits; validator rejects non-finite dimensions, non-unit physics scale, cross-scene actor/anchor references and seat data inconsistent with combined clearance. In Edit Mode, persistent profile contains no scene object references. Repeated installation validates existing anchors and never rewrites adjusted positions.
