## Proposed Technical Implications

- Evaluate ground-level silhouettes, close surface treatment, ground contact and distance readability.
- Include low directional light in later visual evaluation, with light parameters recorded rather than treated as fixed lore constants.
- Separate formation recipes from regional placement so unresolved geography can evolve independently.
- Use a simple open inspection surface initially. Do not invent final regional dimensions or stratigraphy.

These implications are engineering proposals informed by the references, not source-canon facts. No source repository files were modified or copied wholesale.
