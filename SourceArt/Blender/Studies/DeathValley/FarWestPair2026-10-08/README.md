# Further west and northwest sources

Editable sources are `west_far01.blend`, `northwest_far01.blend`, `combined01.blend` and the junction pilot `Pilot01.blend`. All were built, saved, reopened and visually inspected with Blender 5.2.2 LTS using metre units, the fixed projected origin and packed geographic imagery.

The individual sections each contain 256 meshes with 2 m interiors and complete 1 m borders. The combined review contains 2,560 meshes at 4 m interior spacing with full borders. Retained references are protected from selection. Reopen proofs and immutable prepared resources are under `SourceData/Terrain/DeathValley/FarWestPair2026-10-08/Prepared01/` relative to the checkout root.

The [implementation receipt](../../../../../Docs/DeathValley/FAR_WEST_PAIR_IMPLEMENTATION_2026-10-08.md) records provenance, bounds, render evidence, guarded exports and native Unity validation. Playable ground uses exported heights/imagery and native Unity Terrain; these editable Blender sources remain outside the Unity import boundary.
