# First northern ridge source

Editable sources are `north_ridge01.blend`, `combined01.blend` and `Pilot01.blend`, built, saved and reopened with Blender 5.2.2 LTS. Metre units, fixed projected origin and packed geographic imagery are verified.

The new section has 256 meshes with 2 m interior controls and full 1 m borders. The combined review has 3,328 meshes with 4 m interiors and full borders, preserving the existing rectangle plus its western northern cap. The upper-east space remains unbuilt. Prepared resources and native reopen proofs are under `SourceData/Terrain/DeathValley/NorthRidge2026-10-09/Prepared01/` relative to the checkout root.
